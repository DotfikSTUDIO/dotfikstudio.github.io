The Dotfik Studio extension

Free for Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers. It lets the studio see your pointer, clicks and shortcuts in the tabs you record, so your video zooms and highlights by itself.

Download the extension Version 0.1.0 · 35 KB

Install it

  1. Download the extension with the button above, then unzip it (double-click the file). You'll get a folder called dotfik-studio-extension.
  2. Open your browser's extensions page: type chrome://extensions in the address bar (edge://extensions in Edge, brave://extensions in Brave).
  3. Turn on Developer mode with the switch in the corner of that page.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the dotfik-studio-extension folder.
  5. Click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar and pin Dotfik Studio, so it's one click away.

What it adds

  • Automatic zooms where you click, gliding along with your pointer (or holding still, if you prefer).
  • Click effects and a pointer highlight, so viewers never lose track of what you're doing.
  • Shortcut badges such as ⌘ K or Ctrl + S, shown on screen as you press them.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Alt + Shift + R stops the recording and Alt + Shift + P pauses or resumes it, from any tab (on a Mac: ⌥ ⇧ R and ⌥ ⇧ P).

Private by design. The extension only runs inside your web pages while you're recording, never reads password fields, ignores normal typing unless you ask for it, and can't connect to the internet. What it sees goes to the studio tab on your computer and nowhere else. More about privacy.

Why does it ask to read websites?

To notice your clicks in the tabs you record, the extension needs to run in those pages. Chrome describes that as "read and change data on websites". It asks only when you switch on click tracking, and even then its script is only placed in your pages while a recording runs. You can switch access off at any time on the extension's details page.
