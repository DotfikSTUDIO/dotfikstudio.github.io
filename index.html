Questions people ask

The short answers. There are more on the questions and answers page.

Is it really free, with no watermark?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no watermark and no sign-up. Your computer does the work, so each video costs nothing to make.

Is there a time limit?

No limit is set. The recording is kept in your computer's memory, so very long takes (an hour or more) need plenty of free memory.

Is my recording uploaded?

No. Recording, editing and the MP4 export all happen in your browser, and the recording is erased from the page when you're done.

Can it record the sound from my computer?

Yes. Turn on Record computer sound and tick the audio switch in your browser's sharing window. On a Mac this needs Chrome 141 or newer on macOS 14.2 or newer.

Do I need the Chrome extension?

No. It's optional: it adds automatic zooms, click effects and shortcut badges for the Chrome tabs you record. You can add zooms by hand without it.

Does it work on Safari, Firefox or a phone?

Not yet. It needs Chrome, Edge, Brave or another Chromium browser on a Windows, Mac, Chromebook or Linux computer.
