How to record a presentation with your face

Updated 22 October 2026 · 6-minute read

A recorded presentation with your face beside the slides feels much closer to a real talk than slides with a voice-over. This guide shows the free way to do it for Google Slides, PowerPoint and Canva, and what each app's own recorder offers.

The short answer

Open your slides, start the slideshow, and record that tab or your screen with a screen recorder that adds your camera. In Dotfik Studio, free in Chrome or Edge: pick your camera and microphone, share the slides, press Start recording and present. Afterwards, choose a bubble or a split screen and download an MP4. There's no sign-up and no watermark.

How do you record a presentation with your face?

The method is the same for any slides: present them on your screen and record it with your camera on.

  1. Open your presentation: Google Slides, Canva or PowerPoint for the web in Chrome or Edge, or the PowerPoint app.
  2. Open the studio in another tab and choose your camera and microphone.
  3. Choose what to share. For slides in a browser, pick their tab: only that tab is recorded, so your other tabs, apps and notifications stay out of the video. For the PowerPoint app, share your screen (see the PowerPoint section below).
  4. If your slides play videos with sound, turn on Record computer sound and tick the audio switch in the browser's sharing window: Also share tab audio for a tab, Also share system audio for a screen.
  5. Press Start recording, go to your slides, start the slideshow and present. A small floating bubble shows your camera while you talk.
  6. Stop. Pick a layout and a shape, trim the start and end, and download the MP4.

Your camera is recorded separately, so you decide afterwards whether it floats over the slides as a Bubble or sits beside them in a Split layout. The screen and face guide explains each layout.

How do you record yourself presenting Google Slides?

With Dotfik Studio, open the presentation in Chrome or Edge and share its tab in the studio. Press Start recording, click Slideshow and present. Press Esc to leave the slideshow (Google, checked 22 October 2026), then stop the recording.

Google Slides also has its own recorder. On 20 August 2026, Google announced a Record button on the right of Slides, powered by Google Vids: choose Record video, then Camera & Screen to record yourself and your slides. You then edit and share the video in Google Vids. It works only in Chrome and Edge. Google's announcement lists personal Google accounts as well as Workspace ones, but its help page says it "may only be available on certain work or school accounts" (announcement, help page, checked 22 October 2026).

The older Slides recording has moved to View, then Slides Recording, according to the same announcement. It has a 30-minute limit per recording and saves to a My Drive folder called Slides recordings (Google, checked 22 October 2026).

How do you record a PowerPoint with your face?

In PowerPoint for Microsoft 365, the Record tab can record you with your slides. You can turn your camera and microphone on or off, your speaker notes appear as text at the top of the screen like a teleprompter, and you can export the result as a video. PowerPoint for the web doesn't support recording a slide show (Microsoft support, checked 22 October 2026).

With Dotfik Studio, the tidiest way is PowerPoint for the web: present in its browser tab and share that tab, so only the slides are recorded. With the PowerPoint app, share your entire screen, press Start recording, then start the slide show with From Beginning (Microsoft, checked 22 October 2026). The floating camera bubble stays on top of everything, so in a whole-screen recording it can show up over your slides: move it to a second display if you have one. Sound from the whole screen works in Chrome on Windows; on a Mac it needs Chrome 141 or newer on macOS 14.2 or newer, as the Mac guide explains.

How do you record a presentation on Canva?

Canva's own recorder is in the editor: select Present, then Present and record, Next and Go to recording studio. Canva says it works only when you use Canva on a computer, supports one recording per design, and keeps your recordings in Uploads. A public view link is created automatically, even if you don't save the recording, and exporting long recordings isn't supported, so Canva suggests sharing a link instead (Canva Help, checked 22 October 2026).

If you'd rather have an MP4 file, including for a long talk, use Dotfik Studio: open the design in Chrome or Edge, share its tab, press Start recording and click Present. Nothing is uploaded: the video is made on your computer.

Tips for a better presentation recording

  • Match the shape to your slides. Choose 16:9 or 4:3 in Dotfik Studio so the slides fill the frame. Google Slides shows the size under File, then Page setup (Google), and PowerPoint under Design, then Slide Size, where 16:9 is the default for new presentations (Microsoft, both checked 22 October 2026).
  • Keep your notes off the recording. With two screens, share the one with the slides and keep your notes on the other. PowerPoint's Presenter View shows "the current slide, the next slide, and your speaker notes" (Microsoft), and Google Slides has Presenter view in the menu next to Slideshow (Google, both checked 22 October 2026).
  • Use a split layout for long talks. Split puts you beside the slides instead of over them, so you never hide a chart or a line of text.
  • Cut the pauses. Cut the pauses finds long pauses and cuts them in one click, and Even out my voice keeps your volume steady.
  • Add captions. They help anyone watching with the sound off; see how to add captions.
  • Record in sections. A few short takes are easier to redo than one long one.

Questions people ask

Can I record a presentation on a Chromebook?

Yes. Open your slides in Chrome, open the studio in another tab, share the slides tab with your camera on and download an MP4. The Chromebook's built-in recorder can also show your camera with its Front Camera switch (Google, checked 22 October 2026); the Chromebook guide covers both.

Can I record a presentation without showing my face?

Yes. Choose No camera before you record, or pick the Screen layout afterwards. Your voice and your slides stay in the video.

Is there a time limit?

Dotfik Studio sets no fixed limit, but the recording is kept in your computer's memory, so an hour or more needs plenty of free memory. Google's older Slides recording has a 30-minute limit per recording (Google, checked 22 October 2026).

How do I share the recording with my class?

Download the MP4 and upload it wherever your class is, such as Google Classroom, Google Drive or YouTube. Dotfik Studio has no share links: the video is a file on your computer.

Will videos inside my slides have sound in the recording?

Yes, if you turn on Record computer sound and tick the audio switch when you share. For a tab, the switch is Also share tab audio, which works on every system.

Present your slides as usual and record yourself with them, free, in your browser.
