How to screen record on Windows 11 and 10 with audio

Updated 22 October 2026 · 8-minute read

Windows has more than one screen recorder built in, and each records something different. Here's how to use each one with sound, including your PC's own audio and your camera, and what to do when a recording comes out silent.

The short answer

On Windows 11, press Windows + Shift + R to record with the Snipping Tool, and switch its sound on before you press Start. To include your camera, use Clipchamp (30 minutes per recording) or Dotfik Studio in Edge or Chrome, where you tick Also share system audio for the PC's sound. On Windows 10, use Game Bar, Clipchamp or a browser recorder.

How do you screen record on Windows 11 with the Snipping Tool?

  1. Press Windows + Shift + R (the Windows logo key). Or open Snipping Tool, select Record, then New.
  2. Drag to select the part of the screen you want to record.
  3. In the recording bar, turn on the sound you need: your microphone for a voice-over, your PC's audio for sound from apps and videos.
  4. Select Start, then Stop when you've finished.
  5. Save the video, or select Edit in Clipchamp to edit it.

The steps come from Microsoft's Snipping Tool guide, and recordings are saved in Videos > Screen Recordings (Windows Learning Center; both checked 22 October 2026). Microsoft doesn't describe a camera option: the Snipping Tool records the screen and sound only.

Does Windows 10 have a screen recorder?

Not in the Snipping Tool, going by Microsoft: its guide describes screen recording only for Windows 11, and the Windows 10 section covers screenshots alone (checked 22 October 2026). On Windows 10 you can use Xbox Game Bar, which Microsoft documents for Windows 10 and 11, the Clipchamp app from the Microsoft Store, or a browser recorder such as Dotfik Studio, which works the same on both.

How do you record your screen with Xbox Game Bar?

Game Bar is made for games, but it records apps too.

  1. Open the game or app you want to record.
  2. Press Windows + Alt + R to start, and the same keys again to stop. Or press Windows + G and choose Capture > Start recording.
  3. Find the MP4 in Videos > Captures.

For your PC's sound, open Game Bar's settings and set Audio to record to All; Game only records just the game. Windows + Alt + M turns your microphone on or off while you record, and each clip stops at the maximum length you choose in Settings > Gaming > Captures (Xbox Support: capture settings and Game Bar widgets; Microsoft's Game Bar recording guide; all checked 22 October 2026).

If Game Bar won't record an app, press Windows + G while the app is open and select Enable gaming features for this app (Xbox troubleshooting guide, checked 22 October 2026). It won't record the Windows desktop or File Explorer, though. There it shows "Gaming features aren't available for the Windows desktop or File Explorer", as quoted in a Microsoft Q&A thread (checked 22 October 2026). For those, use one of the other tools.

How do you record your screen and webcam on Windows 11?

Clipchamp, Microsoft's video editor, records your screen and camera together. It might already be installed on Windows 11; if not, it's in the Microsoft Store (Microsoft support, checked 22 October 2026). Two limits matter. Webcam and screen recordings are "currently limited to 30 minutes" (Microsoft support, checked 22 October 2026). And in the personal version, "Audio recording only works with screen recording a browser tab": your microphone is recorded, but the PC's sound only comes from a browser tab (Clipchamp recording guide, checked 22 October 2026).

Dotfik Studio does it in the browser, with no fixed time limit and nothing uploaded. Microsoft Edge is "the default web browser in Windows and a core component of the operating system" (Microsoft support, checked 22 October 2026), so there's nothing to install. Chrome and Brave work too.

  1. Open the studio in Edge or Chrome. There's no account to create.
  2. Choose your camera and microphone, and allow access when the browser asks.
  3. Choose what to share: a window, a browser tab or your whole screen. For the PC's sound, turn on Record computer sound and tick the audio switch in the browser's sharing window: Also share tab audio for a tab, Also share system audio for a window or the screen.
  4. Press Start recording. When you stop, edit the video and press Download MP4.

The camera is recorded separately, so you can move it or switch to a split screen afterwards (see how to record your screen and face). You can add zooms by hand anywhere in the video. With the free browser extension, zooms and click effects happen automatically, but only for clicks in the browser tabs you record, not in desktop apps. Long takes need free memory: up to about 40 MB per minute at 1080p.

Why does my Snipping Tool recorded video have no sound?

Usually because the sound was never switched on. Check these in order:

  1. Turn the sound on before you record. Snipping Tool records sound only when you switch it on in the recording bar. Microsoft added "optionally recording your PC audio and voice overs using a microphone" in version 11.2307.44.0, and the app's settings set the default for new recordings (Windows Insider blog, 31 August 2023, checked 22 October 2026).
  2. Update the Snipping Tool. If you see no sound options, your copy is probably older than that. Open Microsoft Store and check for updates (Microsoft support, checked 22 October 2026).
  3. Let apps use the microphone. Go to Start > Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone and turn on Microphone access, Let apps access your microphone and, for browsers such as Chrome, Let desktop apps access your microphone. On Windows 10 it's Start > Settings > Privacy > Microphone (Microsoft support, checked 22 October 2026).
  4. In Game Bar, choose All. Set Audio to record to All, and make sure the system and microphone sliders in Settings > Gaming > Captures aren't at 0.
  5. In Clipchamp's personal version, share a browser tab when you need the PC's sound.
  6. In a browser recorder, tick the audio switch. If Also share system audio wasn't ticked, Dotfik Studio asks you to choose what to share again and tick it.

Questions people ask

How do I record my screen on a PC?

On Windows 11, press Windows + Shift + R to use the Snipping Tool. On any Windows PC, you can also open Dotfik Studio in Edge or Chrome and record your screen, camera and sound.

How can I record my screen without Game Bar?

Use the Snipping Tool on Windows 11, Clipchamp, or a browser recorder such as Dotfik Studio. Unlike Game Bar, they can record the desktop and File Explorer.

Where do screen recordings go on Windows?

Snipping Tool saves them in Videos > Screen Recordings, and Game Bar in Videos > Captures. A browser recorder such as Dotfik Studio saves the MP4 wherever your browser puts downloads.

Can Windows record my screen and face at the same time?

Yes, with Clipchamp (up to 30 minutes per recording) or Dotfik Studio. Microsoft describes no camera option in the Snipping Tool or Game Bar. See how to record your screen and face.

Is there a time limit on screen recording in Windows?

Clipchamp stops at 30 minutes, and Game Bar at the maximum length set in its capture settings. Microsoft doesn't state a limit for the Snipping Tool, and Dotfik Studio has no fixed limit, though long takes need free memory.
