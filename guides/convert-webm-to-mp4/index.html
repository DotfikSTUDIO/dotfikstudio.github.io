How to convert WebM to MP4 for free, without uploading

Updated 22 October 2026 · 7-minute read

Chromebook screen recordings are WebM files, and so is the copy Clipchamp downloads when you record. This guide explains when you actually need an MP4 and the free ways to convert one, including a way that keeps the video on your computer.

The short answer

Open Dotfik Studio in Chrome or Edge, choose Edit a video file instead and pick your WebM file. In the Background panel choose no background, pick a quality, and press Download MP4. You get an MP4 with H.264 video and AAC sound, made on your computer: nothing is uploaded, and it works on a Chromebook too.

What is a WebM file?

WebM is "an open, royalty-free, media file format designed for the web", according to the WebM Project. Its video is compressed with the VP8 or VP9 codec and its sound with Vorbis or Opus (About WebM, checked 22 October 2026).

You'll often meet it in screen recordings. Chromebooks save recordings as .webm files (Google's Chromebook help), and Clipchamp downloads "a copy of the recording (in WebM format)" (Microsoft support; both checked 22 October 2026). MP4 with H.264 video and AAC sound is the combination Microsoft recommends for PowerPoint, and the one Dotfik Studio exports.

Do you need to convert WebM to MP4?

Not always. It depends on where the video is going:

  • YouTube: WebM is on YouTube's list of supported file formats, so you can upload it as it is (YouTube Help, checked 22 October 2026).
  • Google Drive: Drive lists WebM among the video types it can play, though Google adds that "Not all video types may work on all devices" (Google Drive Help, checked 22 October 2026).
  • PowerPoint: PowerPoint for Windows lists WebM, but PowerPoint for Mac's list doesn't, and Microsoft recommends ".mp4 files encoded with H.264 video and AAC audio" (Microsoft support, checked 22 October 2026).
  • Anywhere else: if an app, a phone or the person you're sending it to can't open the file, convert it.

How do you convert WebM to MP4 without uploading it?

Dotfik Studio is a free video editor that runs in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers on Windows, Mac, Chromebook and Linux computers. It opens the file on your computer and makes the MP4 there too, so the video never leaves it.

  1. Open the studio. There's nothing to install and no account to create.
  2. On the start screen, choose Edit a video file instead and pick your WebM file. On a Chromebook, recordings are in Downloads.
  3. In the Video shape panel, pick the shape that matches your video, such as 16:9 for a widescreen recording. Under Quality, choose the resolution (720p to 4K) and frame rate.
  4. In the Background panel, choose No background: the screen fills the frame, so the picture isn't framed by padding or a coloured backdrop.
  5. Press Download MP4 and save it under a new name, so your original stays as it is.

Erase the recording after downloading is on by default, so the copy in the page is erased once the MP4 is saved. Untick it to make another version first, such as a Short; closing the tab erases it anyway. Nothing is uploaded, and the site's security policy stops the page sending data to any other server: the privacy page explains how.

Can you trim or caption the video on the way?

Yes. The converter is an editor, so you can tidy the video before you download it:

  • Trim the start and end, cut out a section, or speed up a slow part 2×, 4× or 8× (sped-up parts are silent).
  • Blur, pixelate or cover an email address or password for exactly as long as it's on screen.
  • Add zooms by hand, text, arrows, an intro title or your logo.
  • Type captions, show them in the video, or save an .srt file for YouTube. Making captions from your voice works on recordings made in the studio, not on files you open (how to add captions).
  • Make a vertical 9:16 version for Shorts, Reels or TikTok (the Shorts guide).

What other free ways are there?

VLC

VLC is a "free and open source cross-platform multimedia player" for Windows, Mac and Linux (VideoLAN), and it converts files too. VideoLAN's user guide gives these steps (both checked 22 October 2026):

  1. Choose Media > Convert/Save.
  2. Click Add, pick your WebM file and go on to the Convert window.
  3. From the Profile list, choose a profile for MP4 with H.264 video.
  4. Under Destination file, type a name ending in .mp4.
  5. Click Start.

ffmpeg

For technical readers, ffmpeg is "a command line tool to convert multimedia files between formats" (About FFmpeg), licensed under the LGPL (FFmpeg legal page). In a terminal, in the folder that holds your video, run:

ffmpeg -i recording.webm -c:v libx264 -c:a aac recording.mp4

-c:v libx264 picks the H.264 encoder for the picture and -c:a aac the AAC encoder for the sound (ffmpeg documentation). Your copy of ffmpeg must include libx264, which is switched on when ffmpeg is built (codec documentation; all checked 22 October 2026).

Online converters

Many online converters work by uploading your video to their server, converting it there and sending it back. That may be fine for a holiday clip, but think twice when the recording shows emails, student names or work documents: read the site's privacy policy and how long it keeps files first. Does converting WebM to MP4 lose quality?

Converting re-encodes the video: the VP8 or VP9 picture becomes H.264, and Vorbis or Opus sound becomes AAC. ffmpeg's documentation describes this as "re-encoding media streams". Each re-encode can lose a little detail, so convert from the original file rather than from a copy that has already been converted, and pick a resolution at least as high as the original's. Re-encoding also takes time: longer for long videos and on slower computers.

Questions people ask

How do I convert WebM to MP4 on a Chromebook?

Open Dotfik Studio in Chrome, choose Edit a video file instead, pick the recording from Downloads and press Download MP4. Everything happens on the Chromebook, and nothing is uploaded.

Is there a free WebM to MP4 converter with no limit?

Dotfik Studio is free, with no sign-up and no watermark, and it sets no time limit, though a very large file can be too big to open because the work happens in your computer's memory. VLC and ffmpeg are free too.

Does YouTube accept WebM files?

Yes. WebM is on YouTube's list of supported file formats, so you can upload a WebM recording without converting it.

Is WebM better than MP4?

Neither is better everywhere. WebM is an open, royalty-free format made for the web; MP4 with H.264 and AAC is what Microsoft recommends for PowerPoint. Use whichever the place you're sending it to accepts.

Will my recording keep its sound after converting?

Yes. Dotfik Studio keeps the sound and saves it as AAC in the MP4, and you can change its volume before you download.
