How to turn a screen recording into a YouTube Short

Updated 22 October 2026 · 7-minute read

Shorts, Reels and TikTok videos are tall and watched on phones, while a screen recording is wide. This guide shows how to turn one into the other for free, keep the screen readable and stay within each app's limits.

The short answer

Change the video's shape to 9:16 (1080 × 1920 pixels at 1080p), put your camera above or below the screen, and zoom in so text stays readable on a phone. YouTube counts square or vertical videos up to three minutes long as Shorts (checked 22 October 2026). Dotfik Studio does all of this free in Chrome or Edge, with no watermark.

How do you turn a screen recording into a Short?

You need three things: a tall 9:16 frame, a sensible place for your face, and zooms so the screen can be read. With Dotfik Studio it all happens in the browser:

  1. Record your screen in the studio as usual, with your camera on if you want to appear. Already have a video? See the section on existing videos below.
  2. In the editor, choose the 9:16 shape.
  3. Pick a camera layout that suits a tall frame (see the layouts below).
  4. Add zooms on everything people need to read: automatically from your clicks in browser tabs with the free extension, or by hand with Add zoom here.
  5. Trim the start and end, cut the slow parts, and add captions.
  6. Choose 1080p and 30 or 60 fps, then download the MP4. At 1080p, a 9:16 video is 1080 × 1920 pixels.
  7. Upload it. On a computer, sign in to YouTube Studio, click CREATE, then Upload videos (YouTube Help, checked 22 October 2026).

How long can Shorts, Reels and TikTok videos be?

Here's what each platform's own help pages say (checked 22 October 2026):

PlatformLengthShape and size
YouTube Shorts Up to 3 minutes (YouTube) Square or vertical
Instagram Reels Up to 20 minutes, but reels over 3 minutes "won't be recommended to new audiences" (Instagram) Between 1.91:1 and 9:16, with at least 720 pixels and 30 fps (Instagram)
TikTok, uploading in a web browser Up to 30 minutes (TikTok) 720 × 1280 or higher, MP4 or WebM, under 10 GB

One more YouTube rule matters if you add music: Shorts longer than one minute with an active Content ID claim are blocked (YouTube Help, checked 22 October 2026). Only use music you have the rights to.

Which camera layout works best for a vertical video?

Dotfik Studio records your camera separately from the screen, so you choose the layout after recording, and it adapts when you change the shape.

  • Split, with your camera at the top or bottom: the screen and your face each get their own part of the tall frame, so you never cover the screen. A good default when you talk as much as you show.
  • Bubble: your face in a circle, rounded square or rounded rectangle over the screen. Drag it where it hides nothing important, and resize it.
  • Screen: only the screen and your voice, for a faceless Short.
  • Camera: only you, full frame, for a talking-head Short.

The guide to recording your screen and face covers the layouts in more detail.

How do you keep the screen readable on a phone?

A wide screen placed in a tall frame is only as wide as the phone, so text that was fine in a YouTube video can become tiny.

  • Zoom in, and stay zoomed. In a Short it's fine to spend most of the video zoomed in on the part that matters. The zoom guide shows both ways to add zooms.
  • Make text bigger before you record. In Chrome, Ctrl and + (⌘ and + on a Mac) makes everything on a page larger (Chrome Help, checked 22 October 2026). Sharing one tab or window instead of a large monitor helps too.
  • Keep important things away from the edges. Shorts, Reels and TikTok lay their buttons, your name and the post's text over the video, usually along the bottom and the right-hand side. Keep key details and captions towards the middle, and check the result on your own phone.

How do you make a YouTube video and a Short from one recording?

You don't need to record twice.

  1. Finish the 16:9 version for YouTube and press Download. In the download window, turn off Erase the recording after downloading, so the recording stays in the page.
  2. Switch the shape to 9:16 and choose a layout for it, such as a split screen with your camera at the bottom.
  3. Trim it to the best part and check the zooms.
  4. Download again. The recording is erased when you close the tab anyway.

How do you make a Short from a video you already have?

On the studio's start screen, choose Edit a video file instead and open the video: a landscape tutorial, a Mac .mov or a Chromebook WebM recording, for example. Pick 9:16, add zooms by hand, trim it to the part you want and download an MP4. Type the captions for a file you open: making captions from your voice works on the microphone sound of a recording made in the studio.

If the video is already on YouTube, YouTube Studio can cut a Short from it too: open Content, select the video, choose Clips and Shorts, then Create clip or Short (YouTube Help, checked 22 October 2026).

Should a Short have captions?

They're not required, but captions let people follow along with the sound off. Burned-in captions are part of the picture, so they appear on every app you post to. In Dotfik Studio, make captions from your voice or type them, then turn on Show captions in the video. The guide to adding captions has the details.

Questions people ask

How do I turn a horizontal video into a vertical one?

Change its frame to 9:16 in a video editor and decide what fills the extra height: a background, your camera in a split screen, or zooms on the screen. In Dotfik Studio, open the file with Edit a video file instead, choose 9:16 and download an MP4, free.

Can I post the same vertical video to Shorts, Reels and TikTok?

Yes: a 9:16 MP4 at 1080 × 1920 and 30 or 60 fps fits what all three ask for. Keep it to three minutes or less so it counts as a YouTube Short and Instagram can recommend it to new audiences (each platform's help pages, checked 22 October 2026).

How do I make YouTube Shorts from existing videos on a PC?

Open the video in the studio with Edit a video file instead, switch to 9:16, trim it and download an MP4, then upload it in YouTube Studio. If the video is already on YouTube, YouTube Studio's Clips and Shorts can cut a Short from it.

What resolution should a YouTube Short be?

1080 × 1920 pixels, which is 9:16 at 1080p, is a safe choice. TikTok asks for at least 720 × 1280, and Instagram for at least 720 pixels and 30 fps (their help pages, checked 22 October 2026).

Can I make Shorts on a Chromebook?

Yes. The studio runs in Chrome on a Chromebook, so you can record, switch to 9:16 and download an MP4 there. The Chromebook's own recordings are WebM files, which you can open with Edit a video file instead; see the Chromebook guide.
