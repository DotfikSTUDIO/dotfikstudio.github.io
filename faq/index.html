Questions and answers

Updated 22 October 2026

The questions people ask most about Dotfik Studio, the free screen recorder that works in your browser. If yours isn't here, the guides go into more detail.

The basics

Is Dotfik Studio really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no trial and no feature held back. Your computer does the recording and editing, so each video costs nothing to make on our side.

Does it add a watermark to my videos?

No. Your downloaded video carries no watermark, logo or end card unless you add your own.

Do I need an account or to sign up?

No. Open the studio and start recording. There are no accounts at all.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Recording your screen, camera and voice works in the browser on its own. The optional Chrome extension adds automatic zooms, click effects and shortcut badges.

Which computers and browsers does it work on?

Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers on Windows, Mac, Chromebook and Linux computers. Safari and Firefox aren't supported yet, because they lack some of the video tools the studio needs.

Does it work on a phone or an iPad?

Not yet. Recording your screen in a browser needs a computer.

Is there a time limit on recordings?

No limit is set. The recording is kept in your computer's memory while you work, up to about 40 MB per minute at 1080p, so very long takes (an hour or more) need plenty of free memory. Several shorter takes are easier to edit anyway.

Recording

How do I record my screen and my face at the same time?

Choose your camera and microphone in the studio, pick the screen to share and press Start recording. Your camera appears in a bubble you can move, resize or turn into a split screen afterwards. The screen and face guide has the details.

Can it record the sound from my computer, not just my voice?

Yes. Turn on Record computer sound, then tick the audio switch in your browser's sharing window. A tab's sound works on every system; a whole screen's sound works in Chrome on Windows and Chromebooks, and on a Mac with Chrome 141 or newer on macOS 14.2 or newer. See how to screen record on a Mac with sound.

Can I record just one window or one browser tab?

Yes. Your browser's sharing window lets you choose a whole screen, a single window or a single tab.

Can I pause a recording?

Yes, with the pause button. With the extension installed, Alt + Shift + P pauses and resumes from any tab, and Alt + Shift + R stops (on a Mac, ⌥ ⇧ P and ⌥ ⇧ R).

Why does my recording have no sound?

Check that the right microphone is chosen in the studio. For computer sound, both the Record computer sound switch and the audio switch in the browser's sharing window must be on. The Windows, Mac and Chromebook guides cover each system.

Can I edit a video I recorded with another app?

Yes. Choose Edit a video file instead on the studio's start screen to open a video, such as a Chromebook WebM or a Mac MOV recording, then edit it and download an MP4. Its sound counts as computer sound, so captions have to be typed and pause cutting isn't offered. See how to convert WebM to MP4.

Editing

How do the automatic zooms work?

With the free extension, the studio knows where you click inside the Chrome tabs you record, and zooms in there. Anything else, including desktop apps, can be zoomed by hand with Add zoom here. See how to zoom in on a screen recording.

Why is the Chrome extension needed for automatic zooms?

A web page only receives the pixels of what you share. The extension tells the studio where your pointer is and where you click inside the tabs you record. It runs only during a recording and has no internet access.

Can it make captions?

Yes. In recent versions of Chrome, captions are made from your voice on your computer, in nine languages, or you can type them. Show them in the video, save them as an .srt file for YouTube, or both. See how to add captions.

Can I make vertical videos for Shorts, Reels and TikTok?

Yes. Choose the 9:16 shape before you download, and your camera layout adapts. The same recording can also be downloaded as 16:9 for YouTube. See the guide to Shorts and Reels.

Can I hide passwords or personal details?

Yes. Blur, pixelate or cover any area for exactly as long as it's on screen. It stays in place through zooms.

Can I cut mistakes and long pauses?

Yes. Trim the start and end, cut out any part, or speed it up 2×, 4× or 8×. Cut the pauses finds the long silences and removes them in one click.

Can I add background music?

Yes. Add a music file and it fades in and out, repeats to the end if you like, and gets quieter while you talk.

Downloading and sharing

What kind of file do I get?

An MP4 file (H.264 video with AAC sound), which plays almost everywhere. Choose 720p, 1080p, 1440p or 4K, at 24, 30 or 60 frames per second, in six shapes from 16:9 to 9:16.

Can I share a link to my video?

No. Dotfik Studio doesn't host videos, so there are no share links. Upload the MP4 wherever you share things: YouTube, Google Drive, Google Classroom, Slack or email. If instant links matter most, see the Loom comparison.

How do I add my captions to a YouTube video?

Save the captions as an .srt file in the editor, then upload that file in YouTube Studio's subtitles section. The captions guide shows the steps.

Privacy and safety

Is my recording uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording, editing and the MP4 export all happen in your browser. The site's security policy stops the page from sending data to any other server. Read the privacy page.

What happens to my recording when I close the tab?

It's erased, because it only ever lived in that tab. Your browser asks you to confirm before you close a tab that still holds a recording, so download it first. Downloading erases it too (untick that switch to make a second version first), and so does pressing Delete.

What can the Chrome extension see?

Your pointer, clicks and keyboard shortcuts inside the tabs you record, and only while a recording runs. It never reads password fields, ignores normal typing unless you turn it on, and can't connect to the internet.

Can a website record my screen without my permission?

No. Your browser asks every time a site wants to see your screen, you choose what it may see, and the browser shows while sharing is on. See whether an online screen recorder is safe.

It's free with no watermark, works on any computer with Chrome, and never uploads your video, but it has no share links or team features. The comparison sets out the honest pros and cons.
