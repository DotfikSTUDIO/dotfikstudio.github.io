A free Loom alternative without the 5-minute limit

Updated 22 October 2026 · 8-minute read

Loom is built for quick video messages, but its free plan is tight: five minutes a video and 25 videos in all. If you want longer videos, a file you can keep, or nothing uploaded, here's an honest comparison, including where Loom is the better choice. We make Dotfik Studio, so every fact about Loom links to Loom's own pages.

The short answer

Loom's free Starter plan allows 25 videos of up to 5 minutes each, at up to 720p, watched through a Loom link. If you want a finished MP4 with no per-video limit and nothing uploaded, Dotfik Studio is free in Chrome or Edge. Loom is still the better choice for instant share links, comments and team libraries.

Does Loom have a time limit?

On the free Starter plan, yes. Loom says "Loom Starter users have a five-minute recording duration limit": the timer starts at 5:00, counts down, and the recording ends at 0:00. Starter also has a limit of 25 videos, and archived videos count towards it. Loom won't delete your videos when you reach it, but "you just won't be able to record more"; its answer is to upgrade to Business. Video quality on Starter goes up to 720p (How long can I record?, Starter plan FAQ, Loom pricing, all checked 22 October 2026).

Business ($18 per user per month) and Business + AI ($24 per user per month) remove both limits, and Loom says you "save 17% if you pay annually". Dotfik Studio sets no length limit and no limit on the number of videos. The recording is kept in your computer's memory while you edit, up to about 40 MB a minute at 1080p, so an hour-long take needs plenty of free memory.

Where is Loom better?

  • Sharing by link. Copy the video's link and send it: no file to export or upload. Public videos can be watched by anyone with the link, and "no login or Loom account is required" (Loom help, checked 22 October 2026).
  • Conversation around the video. Comments, emoji reactions and viewer insights are all in the free plan, next to the video itself.
  • Team libraries. A team workspace with personal, shared and team libraries, even on Starter.
  • Phones. Loom has apps for iPhone and Android; Dotfik Studio doesn't work on phones or tablets yet.
  • Transcripts in more languages. Loom lists transcriptions in 50+ languages, on the free plan too.

Where is Dotfik Studio better?

  • No 5-minute timer and no 25-video cap. Record a 40-minute lesson or a hundred bug reports.
  • You keep the file. Every recording becomes an MP4 you can upload to YouTube, Google Drive, Google Classroom or Slack. On Loom, "Users on the Starter Free plan can't download videos".
  • Real editing, free. Trim, cut mistakes, speed up slow parts, remove long pauses in one click, add zooms, callouts and captions, and blur an email address or password. Loom lists trimming from Business.
  • Nothing uploaded. Recording, editing and the MP4 export happen on your computer, with no account, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Sharper video. Export up to 4K at 60 fps, against 720p on Loom's free plan.

Are Loom videos private?

They can be, but check the setting. Loom's help centre lists three access levels. Public videos "can be viewed by anyone who has the link to your video; no login or Loom account is required". Open to workspace videos can be watched and found by anyone logged in to your workspace. Restricted videos can only be watched by people whose email you've added. New videos are Public unless your workspace admins change the default (Use Loom's privacy settings, checked 22 October 2026).

Whatever the setting, Loom stores the video so that others can watch it. With Dotfik Studio, nothing leaves your computer until you choose where to upload the MP4. Our guide on whether online screen recorders are safe shows how to check any recorder yourself.

Is Loom free for students and teachers?

Anyone can use the free Starter plan, with its 5-minute and 25-video limits. Beyond that, education pricing is a discount rather than free. Loom's education page says "Qualified educators get unlimited recordings and access to premium features at 75% off", and its Get started button leads to Atlassian's classroom licence request. Atlassian lists Loom Business and Business + AI at 75% off for "eligible collegiate students, graduate students, and teachers", and at 50% off for eligible academic institutions (Loom for education, Atlassian licensing, checked 22 October 2026).

Dotfik Studio is free for everyone, students included, with no account. For classroom features such as questions inside videos, read our Castify (Screencastify) comparison.

What other free Loom alternatives are there?

It depends on which part of Loom you need:

  • Cap calls itself "the open source alternative to Loom". It's a desktop app for Mac, Windows and Linux; Studio Mode records to your device with no time limit, and free share links are capped at 5 minutes. The free version is for personal use only (Cap pricing, checked 22 October 2026).
  • Screenity is a free, open-source Chrome extension that keeps recordings on your device. Share links and its editor are part of the paid plan, $10 a month or $120 billed yearly (screenity.io, checked 22 October 2026).
  • Castify, formerly Screencastify, has share links on its free plan: 10 videos of up to 30 minutes, with a watermark (Castify pricing, checked 22 October 2026).
  • Dotfik Studio has no share links, but no limits, no watermark and a full editor, for free.
  • OBS Studio and the recorders built into your computer save files to your computer, and you share them yourself.

How do you share a video without Loom?

  1. Record in the studio: screen, camera and voice, in one take.
  2. Trim it, add captions if you like, and download the MP4.
  3. Upload it where your viewers already are, such as Google Drive, YouTube or Slack, and share that link.

It's one more step than Loom, and you won't see comments or viewer insights. In return, you choose where the video lives and who can see it.

Questions people ask

Can I download my Loom videos on the free plan?

No. Loom's help centre says users on the Starter Free plan can't download videos. Downloads, as MP4 files, are available to admins and creators on Business plans.

What happens when I reach 25 videos on Loom?

Loom keeps your videos, but you can't record any more on Starter; Loom's help centre suggests upgrading to a paid Business account. Archived videos count towards the 25.

Does Dotfik Studio have share links like Loom?

No. You download an MP4 and upload it wherever you like, such as YouTube, Google Drive, Google Classroom or Slack. There's no cloud library.

Can I record more than 5 minutes for free?

Yes. Dotfik Studio has no fixed limit, as long as your computer has free memory for long takes. Castify's free plan allows up to 30 minutes per video, and Clipchamp's screen recordings stop at 30 minutes.

Is Dotfik Studio really free?

Yes. There's no paid plan, no account and no watermark, and nothing you record is uploaded.
