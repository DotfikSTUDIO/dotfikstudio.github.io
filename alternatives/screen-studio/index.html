A free Screen Studio alternative for Windows, Chromebook and Linux

Updated 22 October 2026 · 8-minute read

Screen Studio makes screen recordings look polished, with zooms that follow your clicks. But it only runs on a Mac, and it's a subscription. If you're on Windows or a Chromebook, or you'd rather not pay, here's an honest look at what a free browser recorder can and can't match. We make Dotfik Studio, so every fact about Screen Studio links to its own website.

The short answer

Screen Studio is a paid, Mac-only app ($29 a month, or $9 a month on the yearly plan) that records your display, a window or an area and zooms in where you clicked. Dotfik Studio is free and runs in Chrome or Edge on Windows, Chromebook, Linux and Mac, but its automatic zoom only follows clicks in Chrome web pages, with its extension; elsewhere, you add zooms by hand.

Is Screen Studio free?

No. Screen Studio's website lists two plans, each with every feature included: $29 a month on the monthly plan, or $9 a month on the yearly plan. It lists no free plan. Students and educators get 40% off. One-time licences are "no longer available for new purchases", although licences bought in the past still work (screen.studio, checked 22 October 2026).

Dotfik Studio is free, with no paid plan, no account and no watermark.

Is there a Screen Studio for Windows?

No. Screen Studio says it is "made for Mac and nothing else", and its FAQ says there are "no near-future plans to add Windows support". It recommends macOS 13.1 or later (screen.studio, checked 22 October 2026).

On Windows there are two kinds of alternative. Dotfik Studio runs in Chrome, Edge or Brave, so there's nothing to install, and it works the same on a Chromebook or a Linux laptop. Cap is an open-source desktop app for Windows, Mac and Linux that can "generate zooms from your recorded clicks". It's free for personal use, and commercial use needs a paid licence, from $29 per licence per year or $58 once (Cap pricing, checked 22 October 2026). If most of your demos happen in desktop apps rather than a browser, compare Cap too. The comparison of free screen recorders covers more options.

Where is Screen Studio better?

  • Automatic zoom from your clicks. Screen Studio records "your whole display, one window, or just the area that matters", then "looks at what you did while recording, like where you clicked, and zooms in on the moments that matter". Dotfik Studio's automatic zoom can't see clicks in desktop apps.
  • Cursor polish. It records your screen without the cursor, then adds the cursor back, so you can smooth its movement and change its size and style after recording.
  • Editing by transcript. "Delete a sentence to cut it", and clear filler words and long pauses in one pass.
  • iPhone and iPad recording, framed in a matching device bezel.
  • Share links when you want them, for videos up to 30 minutes, uploaded only when you create one.

On privacy, the two are closer than you might expect: Screen Studio's FAQ says your recordings "are stored and processed locally on your computer", and its cloud features are optional.

Where is Dotfik Studio better?

  • It's free, with no account, no watermark and no paid plan.
  • It works on Windows, Chromebooks and Linux as well as Macs, in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers. Safari, Firefox, phones and tablets aren't supported yet.
  • Nothing to install to start. You record from the studio page; the extension is only needed for automatic zoom, click effects and shortcut badges.
  • No cloud at all. Recording, editing and the MP4 export happen on your computer, and the site's security policy stops the page from sending data to any other server.

Both offer several camera layouts, both make vertical videos, and both can turn your voice into captions on your own computer. Our guides show how in Dotfik Studio: screen and face, Shorts and Reels and captions.

How does automatic zoom work in each?

Screen Studio records your clicks along with the screen, then "automatically detects the click positions and zooms in on those areas". If there's no click, it can't zoom automatically, and you add a zoom by hand (Screen Studio's auto zoom guide, checked 22 October 2026).

Dotfik Studio needs the free Chrome extension to see where you click. The extension lives in web pages, so it notices clicks, pointer movement and shortcuts inside Chrome web pages, whether you share a tab, a window or the whole screen, but not in desktop apps. It isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet; installing it takes about a minute with Load unpacked. In desktop apps, or without the extension, use Add zoom here at the right moment on the timeline. It works in any recording, and you can move, retime or delete every zoom. The guide to zooming in on a screen recording shows both ways.

Which should you choose?

  • Choose Screen Studio if you work on a Mac, record demos of desktop apps often, and the subscription is worth the editing time it saves you.
  • Choose Dotfik Studio if you're on Windows, a Chromebook or Linux, if what you demo happens mostly in the browser, or if you want a free tool with no account. On a Mac, read how to screen record on a Mac first.
  • Compare Cap as well if you'd rather have a desktop app on Windows with zooms made from your clicks, and you're recording for personal use or can pay for a licence.

Questions people ask

Can I buy Screen Studio once instead of subscribing?

Not any more. Screen Studio says one-time licences are no longer available for new purchases; licences bought before still work, and their owners can renew them for the latest updates.

Does Screen Studio have a student discount?

Yes. Its FAQ says students and educators get 40% off, automatically with an email address from a university or other educational institution, or by sending proof of student status such as a student ID.

Can Dotfik Studio zoom in automatically in desktop apps?

No. Automatic zoom follows clicks inside Chrome web pages, using the free extension, whether you share a tab, a window or the whole screen. In desktop apps, add zooms by hand with Add zoom here, which works in any recording.

Do I need the Dotfik Studio extension?

Only for automatic zoom, click effects and shortcut badges in Chrome web pages. Recording your screen, camera and voice, editing, and adding zooms by hand all work without it.

Does Screen Studio upload my recordings?

Not by default. Its FAQ says recordings are stored and processed on your computer; a shareable link uploads that video when you create one, and Screen Studio asks before the first upload.
