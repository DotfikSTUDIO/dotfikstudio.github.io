A free Screencastify alternative with no watermark

Updated 22 October 2026 · 7-minute read

Screencastify, now called Castify, has tools made for classrooms: it connects to Google Classroom and can put questions inside your videos. But its free plan adds a watermark and stops at 10 videos. Here's an honest comparison for teachers and students, including what Castify does better and what to check on a school Chromebook. We make Dotfik Studio, so every fact about Castify links to Castify's own pages.

The short answer

Screencastify is now called Castify. Its free plan allows 10 videos of up to 30 minutes, at up to 1080p, with a Castify watermark. Dotfik Studio is a free alternative with no watermark, no limit on the number of videos and nothing uploaded, which helps with student privacy. It has no share links, questions or classroom tools, though.

What happened to Screencastify?

On 19 March 2026, the company announced that "Screencastify officially becomes Castify", a wider set of tools that includes video editing, AI documents and video assignments, and that existing users "will see their favorite tools rebranded within the new, expanded workspace" (the company's press release, checked 22 October 2026). The recorder still carries the old name: Castify's site says "Castify and the Screencastify video recording browser extension run directly in your browser", in Chrome or Edge (castify.com, checked 22 October 2026).

Is Screencastify free for educators?

There's a free plan that anyone can use, teachers included, but Castify's pricing page lists no separate free plan for educators. On the free plan you can "create or upload up to 10 videos", record up to 30 minutes per video at up to 1080p, and every video "Has Castify watermark". You can keep, watch and share those first 10 videos for as long as you like, and you get 5 bonus videos for each person you invite, up to 4 invites. MP4 exports are limited to 5 videos (Castify pricing, checked 22 October 2026).

The Starter plan costs $7 per user per month, billed annually ($19 billed monthly), allows unlimited videos up to 60 minutes and removes the watermark on new videos. Pro costs $10 per user per month, billed annually ($25 billed monthly), and allows recordings up to 180 minutes, unlimited MP4 exports, and captions and AI-generated questions on every video (the free and Starter plans get them on 5 videos). Schools get custom pricing on the "Enterprise & EDU" plan.

Where is Castify better for classrooms?

  • Learning platforms. It integrates with Google Classroom, Canvas and Schoology, with grade sync for Canvas and Schoology on Pro.
  • Questions inside videos. Questions and polls are included even on the free plan, and AI can write them for you (on 5 videos, or every video on Pro).
  • Collecting student videos. Castify Submit lets you request and collect videos from students.
  • Share links and analytics. Students watch from a link, and you can see viewer analytics (limited on the free plan).
  • Tools for schools. The Enterprise & EDU plan adds student restrictions, admin analytics and commenting controls.

Where is Dotfik Studio better?

  • No watermark and no limit on the number of videos, for free.
  • Every video downloads as an MP4, ready for Google Drive, Google Classroom or YouTube.
  • Nothing is uploaded. There's no account, no analytics and no tracking. The recording is erased from the page after download by default, and always when you close the tab. Dotfik Studio never receives your students' faces or voices.
  • Full editing, free. Trim, cut, speed up, zoom, blur private details, add callouts and title cards, and make captions on your own computer.
  • Every shape. One recording can become a 16:9 lesson video, a 4:3 video for slides or a 9:16 clip for a phone.

What it doesn't have: share links, questions, viewer analytics, platform integration or admin controls. You download the MP4 and post it to Google Classroom or Google Drive yourself, like any other file.

Will it work on a school Chromebook?

Usually, yes. Dotfik Studio runs in Chrome on a Chromebook with nothing to install. But a school's administrator controls the Chromebooks it manages, and Chrome's enterprise policies let them (Chrome Enterprise policy list, checked 22 October 2026):

  • Turn off screen capture for websites. With the "Allow or deny screen capture" policy set to deny, "any calls to screen-share APIs will fail with an error" unless the administrator allows that site. Browser recorders, Dotfik Studio included, then can't record.
  • Block developer mode or unpacked extensions. The Dotfik Studio extension isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet, so it's installed with Load unpacked in developer mode. Administrators can switch developer mode off, and when every extension is blocked, "All unpacked extensions are blocked". Without the extension you can still record, use your camera and add zooms by hand.
  • Switch off the built-in recorder. One option reads "Do not allow users to take screenshots or video recordings".

If recording is blocked, ask your teacher or IT team rather than looking for a way round it. Our Chromebook recording guide covers the built-in recorder too.

How do you share a Dotfik Studio video with your class?

  1. Open the studio in Chrome and record your screen, camera and voice.
  2. Trim the start and end, add captions if you like, and choose a shape.
  3. Download the MP4. It's saved to your computer, and by default the recording is then erased from the page.
  4. Upload it to Google Drive or Google Classroom, as you would any other file.

Questions people ask

Is Screencastify the same as Castify?

Yes. Screencastify became Castify in March 2026. The recorder extension still carries the Screencastify name and sits inside Castify's wider set of tools.

Does Castify still have a free plan?

Yes: 10 videos of up to 30 minutes each, at up to 1080p, with a Castify watermark and up to 5 MP4 exports, according to its pricing page (checked 22 October 2026).

Can I add quiz questions to my videos with Dotfik Studio?

No. Dotfik Studio makes an MP4 file. If you want questions inside the video, Castify includes questions and polls even on its free plan.

Can students use Dotfik Studio without an account?

Yes. There's no sign-up, and the recording stays on the computer until the student downloads the MP4 and hands it in.

How do I turn a Chromebook screen recording into an MP4?

The built-in recorder saves WebM files. Open one in Dotfik Studio with Edit a video file instead and export it as MP4; the WebM to MP4 guide shows the steps.
