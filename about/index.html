About Dotfik Studio

A free screen recorder and video editor that runs entirely in your browser. Record your screen, camera and voice; get automatic zooms, click effects and captions; download an MP4 for YouTube, Shorts, Reels or TikTok. No sign-up, no watermark, and nothing is uploaded.

Why it exists

Polished screen recordings, the kind with smooth zooms, a camera bubble and a tidy background, used to mean a paid Mac app or uploading your video to somebody else's cloud. Dotfik Studio does that work inside your browser instead. It is free, it runs on Windows, Mac, Chromebook and Linux computers, and your video never leaves your computer.

How it works

  • Recording uses your browser's own screen sharing, camera and microphone. You choose what to share, and the browser shows that it's being shared.
  • Video is encoded on your computer with the browser's built-in video encoder (WebCodecs), and kept in the tab's memory while you edit.
  • Editing (zooms, backgrounds, your camera layout, callouts and captions) is drawn frame by frame by the same code that shows the preview, so the download looks exactly like what you saw.
  • Captions are made by your browser's on-device speech recognition, so your voice is never sent to an online service.
  • The download is an MP4 file made on your computer. When it's saved, the recording is erased from the page, unless you choose to keep it for a second version.
  • The optional Chrome extension tells the studio where you point and click in the tab you record, so zooms can follow you. It works only during a recording and has no internet access.

Private by design, not by promise

The website comes with a security policy that stops it from sending anything to any other server, so even a bug couldn't upload your recording. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Read the privacy page for the details.

Who makes it

Dotfik Studio is made by Dotfik and has been online since 19 October 2026. It is built for people who teach on video: software tutorials, product demos, course lessons, bug reports and YouTube Shorts.
