Your recordings stay yours

Dotfik Studio records, edits and exports your video entirely inside your browser. We never receive your recordings, and we don't keep anything about you.

The short version: nothing you record is uploaded, stored on a server or shared with anyone. When you're done, it's erased.

What happens to a recording

  • It stays in this browser tab. Your screen, camera and voice are recorded into the tab's own memory. They are never written to our servers, because there are no servers involved: this website is a set of plain files.
  • It's erased when you're done. Downloading your finished video erases the recording from the page straight away (you can untick that if you want to make a second version first). Pressing Delete erases it at any time, and closing the tab erases it too.
  • Your video is made on your computer. Zooms, layouts and the final MP4 are all produced by your own computer. The finished file goes straight to your Downloads folder.
  • Pictures and music you add stay local too. A background picture, your logo or a music track are read from your computer and kept in the same memory, then erased with the recording.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no fonts or scripts from other companies.

Like every website, the company that hosts these files may keep short technical logs of the requests it receives (for example, that a page was downloaded at a certain time). Those logs can never contain your recordings, because your recordings never leave your computer.

A lock on the door

The site tells your browser, through a security rule called a Content Security Policy, that its pages may only load files from this website and may not connect to any other server. Even if something went wrong, your browser would refuse to send a recording anywhere else.

Camera, microphone and screen

Your browser asks your permission before the studio can use your camera, microphone or screen, and shows that they're in use. The studio only uses them while you set up and make a recording. You can withdraw permission at any time in your browser's site settings.

The Chrome extension

  • The extension lets the studio know where your pointer is, where you click and which shortcuts you press in the browser tabs you record, so it can add zooms, click effects and shortcut badges.
  • It only runs inside your web pages while a recording is running, and only sends what it sees to the studio tab on your own computer.
  • It never reads what you type into password fields, and by default it ignores normal typing altogether: only shortcuts such as ⌘ C or Ctrl + C are shown.
  • It reads the size and position of your screens, only so that clicks land in the right place when you record a whole screen.
  • It remembers only two things, inside Chrome on your computer: whether a recording is running, and the studio's address, so its button can take you back to the studio.
  • It has no permission to connect to the internet and contains no tracking of any kind.
